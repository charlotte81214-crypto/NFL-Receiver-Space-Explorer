# 60-Second Demo Script

A plain-English walkthrough for presenting the tool.

---

## The problem (5 seconds)

"When reviewing a pass play, it's easy to judge the quarterback's decision
knowing how it ended. This tool lets you look at the receiver spacing picture
first — before the outcome is revealed."

---

## Select a play (10 seconds)

"We have three plays from the TB vs DAL game on September 9, 2021.
Let's start with Play 97 — a pass from Tom Brady."

- Select **Play 97** from the dropdown.
- Click **Coach Review** to enter review mode.
- Point out: the play description and result are hidden. The field shows
  routes running, each receiver in a different colour.

---

## Freeze and discuss (20 seconds)

"I'll hit Play and let it run to the release frame — it stops automatically."

- Click **▶ Play**. Animation runs from snap to release, then pauses.
- Point to the field: "The coloured dots are receivers. The dashed lines
  show the distance to the nearest defender at that moment."
- Point to the table: "This column shows whether that space was growing or
  shrinking over the last five frames. An arrow up means increasing distance."
- Ask the audience: "Who has the most nearby space here?
  Who is losing space?"

---

## Select a receiver (10 seconds)

"Let's focus on one. Click any receiver's name in the table,
or use the buttons in the Coach Review panel."

- Click a receiver button (e.g. Evans).
- Point out: their dot brightens, the others dim, their distance line
  is highlighted. The table row highlights too.
- "The selection doesn't grade the choice as right or wrong.
  It just focuses the picture."

---

## Reveal (10 seconds)

"Now let's see what actually happened."

- Click **Reveal actual target & outcome**.
- The play description and result appear.
- "The tool matched the target from the play description text.
  If the description was ambiguous, it says so."

---

## Practical use and limitation (5 seconds)

"This is useful for opening a discussion about route spacing — not for
deciding where the quarterback should have thrown. The nearest-defender
distance doesn't tell us about passing lanes, how much pressure the QB
was under, or whether a receiver was actually reachable."

- Click **Reset** to start again, or switch to another play.

---

## Close

"This is a prototype on three plays from one 2021 game.
The interaction — observe, freeze, discuss, reveal — is the contribution."
